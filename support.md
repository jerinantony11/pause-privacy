# Pause Support

Pause helps you use your phone on purpose. This page covers setup, the
most common problems, and how subscriptions work. If you can't find an
answer here, email us. We read every message.

## Requirements
Pause works on iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26.2 or later. No account
is needed.

## Getting started

Pause has two tools. They work independently: you can use either one, or
both on the same app.

**Pause — a moment before you open an app.** This uses an automation in
Apple's Shortcuts app. In Pause, go to **Settings › Setup & help** and
follow "Setup steps, with pictures". It takes about three minutes:

1. Open **Shortcuts** and go to the **Automation** tab.
2. Tap **New Automation** (or **+**), then choose **App**.
3. Choose the app, tick **Is Opened**, and select **Run Immediately**.
   Make sure **Notify When Run** is off.
4. Tap **Create New Shortcut**, search for **Pause**, and add the
   **Pause before opening** action.
5. Tap the blue **App** box inside the action and choose the same app.
6. Save with the blue tick.

**Block — protected hours.** In Pause, go to the **Block** tab, tap
**Allow Screen Time**, pick the apps, and set your hours. During those
hours the apps won't open, and their websites are blocked in Safari and
other browsers. Every block ends on its own when your hours are over.

## Troubleshooting

**Pause doesn't appear when I open an app**
- The blue **App** box in the "Pause before opening" action was left
  empty. This is the most common cause. The automation runs but does
  nothing. Open the automation, tap the box, and choose the app.
- The automation is set to ask before running. It must be set to
  **Run Immediately** with **Notify When Run** off.
- You're on the free plan, which guards one app. Other apps you've set up
  open straight through. The plan card at the top of Settings shows which
  app is free.
- You have pause hours set to "Pause stays out" running right now. Check
  the Pause tab.
- You reopened the app within a few seconds of going in, or within
  30 seconds of switching away. Pause treats that as the same visit, on
  purpose.

**A block didn't start**
- Screen Time access is off. iOS can turn it off after an update. Check
  **Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access** on your
  device.
- The block hours are shorter than 15 minutes. iOS silently skips blocks
  that short.
- More than 8 sets of block hours are switched on. iOS only allows 8 at
  a time. Turn one off.
- The block hours are switched off. Each set has its own switch on the
  Block tab.
- The app is past the free plan's limit. The free plan blocks one app,
  the same one it pauses.

**A website still opens during a block**
- A page that was already open before the block started can stay on
  screen. Close the browser fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and
  open it again.
- The site uses another address, like a short link or a mirror. You can
  add it in **Block › Websites** with Pause Plus.

**I need an app during block hours**
There's always a way through. From the block screen you can get one
minute of access to a single app after a 5-minute wait. For a real
emergency, the app has an emergency stop that ends the block after a
15-minute wait.

**How do I remove Pause completely?**
Delete your Shortcuts automations for Pause, then delete the app. Deleting
the app also removes its Screen Time access and ends any blocks.

## Pause Plus and subscriptions

Pause is free to use with one app. Pause Plus lets you pause and block
every app you choose, block whole Screen Time categories, and add extra
websites. It's available monthly or yearly, each with a one-month free
trial.

- **Restore a purchase:** tap **Restore Purchases** in Pause's Settings
  or on the Pause Plus screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you
  bought with.
- **Cancel or change plan:** open **Settings › [your name] ›
  Subscriptions** on your device, choose Pause, and tap Cancel or pick
  another plan. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid
  being charged for the next period.
- **Free trial:** you won't be charged if you cancel before the trial
  ends. Pause can remind you shortly before it does.
- **Refunds:** payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request
  one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your data
Pause has no account, no server, no analytics, and no ads. Everything
stays on your device. You can export or erase your history at any time in
**Settings › Your data**.
