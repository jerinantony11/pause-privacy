# Pause Privacy Policy

Last updated: [11 September 2026]

Pause is designed to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect
Nothing. Pause has no account, no analytics, and no ads. We do not collect,
store, sell, or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device
Your focus schedules, the apps you choose, and your history of pauses are
stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. This information is
never sent to us or anyone else.

## Screen Time
If you turn on focus sessions, Pause uses Apple's Screen Time API to block
the apps you choose during the times you set. Apple gives Pause only
anonymous tokens for your selections, not the names of your apps or what you
do in them. This stays on your device. You can turn off Pause's Screen Time
access at any time in Settings > Screen Time.

## Purchases
Pause Plus is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's
privacy policy covers that transaction.
